# Qwen U field-alignment contract

Last updated: 2026-09-07 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Purpose

This contract replaces immediate driving-task tuning with an explicit
alignment phase. Its question is whether Qwen can identify a frame-local U
record, read the requested physical field, associate that record with the
corresponding camera region, and explain the physical state before it is asked
to choose an action or trajectory.

The physical U computation remains deterministic and task-agnostic. No hidden
actor label, collision outcome, action, or trajectory may be used to train the
U encoder in this phase.

## A0: language and numeric upper bound

A0 contains no continuous U token and performs no training. It evaluates the
20 frozen route-disjoint V1k validation/held-out examples in two modes:

1. text only; and
2. the three native 1600 x 900 RGB views plus identical text.

The text states the exact `Fxx.route_weight_mean` value, defines
`route_weight_mean` as route overlap in `[0,1]`, gives the `>= 0.2` rule, and
requests exactly `ON_ROUTE` or `OFF_ROUTE`. The score is the lower mean
answer-token negative log likelihood between the two candidates. A0 therefore
tests language/numeric competence and image-context interference only. It
cannot establish continuous-U alignment, visual grounding, planning, or
safety.

The preregistered diagnostic expectation is at least 90% overall accuracy and
at least 80% within each split/label slice for both modes. Failure blocks
interpretation of a later soft-token result until the prompt/runtime issue is
understood. Passing only establishes an upper bound.

A0 is complete. On the frozen 20 examples, text-only and RGB-plus-text each
scored `20/20`, with every validation/held-out and ON/OFF slice at `5/5`.
Independent audit passed with zero failures. This activates the A1 corpus
audit; it does not relax any later alignment or safety gate.

## A1: field-level U-language alignment

The prior one-row projector mixed all 23 physical scalars into one anonymous
soft token and received only the final answer loss. A1 must instead preserve
four identities before learned mixing:

- record type: global or frontier;
- frame-local slot: `G00--G15` or `F00--F31`;
- physical field name;
- continuous value.

Each scalar input is represented by the sum or concatenation of a record-type
embedding, slot embedding, field embedding, and continuous scalar basis. A
small field-query transformer then cross-attends to these typed scalar inputs.
This module is Q-Former-like in mechanism, but its responsibility is narrower:
it may retrieve, organize, and translate physical fields; it may not predict a
hidden actor, risk label, driving action, or trajectory.

The first implementation should use one query per valid U record, retain the
frame-local slot identity at its output, and emit one aligned token per record
for Qwen. Auxiliary readout heads used only during alignment must reconstruct
or classify the underlying fields. They are not part of the driving policy.

### Automatically generated supervision

The deterministic U builder can produce dense labels without human annotation.
Training examples must cover multiple fields and randomized questions rather
than only `route_weight_mean >= 0.2`:

- slot identification and valid-row masking;
- field-name selection;
- normalized value regression;
- value buckets with distribution-aware boundaries;
- comparisons between two fields or records;
- randomized threshold questions;
- autogenerated literal descriptions of position, visibility, observation
  age, route overlap, stopping exposure, and urgency;
- masked-field reconstruction;
- alignment between a U-record representation and its literal text
  description.

Exact regression, bucket, and classification metrics must be reported
separately. A single final-answer accuracy is insufficient because it cannot
localize an address, field, numeric, or language failure.

## A2: U-visual spatial alignment

Each frontier has an ego-frame 3D center and source camera calibration. A2
projects that frontier back into every camera in which its boundary is valid
and records camera identity, pixel or patch coordinates, visibility, and an
auditable overlay. The target is the visible occluding boundary associated
with the unknown space, not an invented image of a hidden actor.

A2 supervision includes:

- `Fxx -> camera/patch` retrieval;
- marked camera region `-> Fxx` retrieval;
- positive U-record/ROI pairs versus spatial negatives from the same frame;
- consistency of ego-frame left/right/forward relations with projected image
  locations;
- literal questions joining one image region to one or more physical fields.

The alignment split remains route-disjoint. Any same-frame negatives must be
scored according to their real correspondence; there is no forced
opposite-answer or pair-flip loss.

## Training stages and capacity

1. Train the field-query module and auxiliary field/visual heads while Qwen is
   frozen. Use dense A1/A2 supervision over many frames rather than repeating
   70 binary examples.
2. Once field and spatial readouts generalize, instruction-tune the aligned
   tokens with Qwen using a materially larger but still bounded adapter. The
   initial capacity candidate is rank-32 LoRA over the eight released
   full-attention layers' `q/k/v/o` modules. MLP adaptation is not enabled
   automatically.
3. Mix retained driving/general VQA examples during broader tuning if needed
   to measure and limit catastrophic forgetting.

The exact frame count, field/bucket balance, query-transformer width/depth,
and acceptance thresholds for A1/A2 require a no-training corpus audit before
optimization. They must not be chosen after inspecting held-out outcomes.

The first no-training audit is complete on the route-disjoint 90-frame pilot:
70/10/10 unique train/validation/held-out routes yield 3,360/480/441 valid
records, and all 90 frames contain usable calibration for the three native
Qwen cameras. Training-only field distributions show that one uniform bucket
scheme would be artificial. Five-quantile classification is used only when
all four training quantile edges are distinct; constant and zero-heavy fields
use their literal type, exact-value, or zero/nonzero targets instead. Continuous
regression and explicit extrapolation slices remain mandatory.

A1a physical reconstruction is complete. The field-query bridge passes both
route-disjoint evaluation splits with macro MAE below `0.016`, worst-field MAE
below `0.039`, and exact type/slot accuracy. Independent reload audit also
confirms its Qwen-facing projection and boundary embeddings remain zero. This
passes the record-retention prerequisite only; A1 remains open until frozen
Qwen can consume the bridge under explicit field-language supervision.

## Evidence ladder

The allowed progression is:

```text
A0 textual upper bound
  -> A1 field/address/numeric alignment
  -> A2 U-to-camera spatial alignment
  -> structured physical reasoning with continuous U
  -> longitudinal driving-task tuning
  -> Planning Expert and closed-loop evaluation
```

Failure at one rung does not authorize claiming failure of the later concept.
Driving, Planning Expert, NAVSIM, and Bench2Drive closed-loop experiments remain
paused until continuous-U field and visual alignment pass on route-disjoint
data.
